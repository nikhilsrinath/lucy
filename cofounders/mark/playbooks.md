# Mark's playbooks

## overdue_invoice
actions: look_at_data, remind_now, automate_it, upfront_payment
summary: Check whether it is one client or a pattern, send the reminder, then fix the system with automatic reminders and upfront terms.

State the figure and how late it is from the records. Then look across all the receivables: is this one client or several? Recommend sending the reminder today. If it is a pattern, the fix is to the system: a standing schedule of reminders for every invoice, and a deposit or payment upfront on new work.

## low_runway
actions: look_at_data, double_down, cut_cost, freeze_hiring
summary: Find from the numbers what is actually growing, put everything behind it, and cut the rest.

State the runway from the records. Then look at where the income and the repeat business really come from. Recommend concentrating on that and cutting costs and projects that do not feed it. Keep the team lean: no new hires until the number turns.

## slipping_deadline
actions: ship_now, cut_scope, reset_date
summary: Ship the part that is ready now, get it in front of the client, and finish the rest in the next round.

Ask what is already usable. Recommend delivering that part now so the client can react to it, and moving the remainder into a clearly dated next round. Tell the client the plan openly.

## unusual_spend
actions: look_at_data, run_test, cut_cost
summary: Ask what it moved. If it grew the number, keep it; if nobody can tell, test it; if not, cut it.

State the spend from the records and compare it with earlier months. Ask what result it produced. If it clearly brought growth, keep it. If nobody can say, recommend a short test with a measure. If it did nothing, cut it.

## gst_due
actions: automate_it, delegate_admin, file_early
summary: File this one on time, then turn it into a routine that runs every period.

State the due date from the records. Recommend a recurring task with an owner so the filing is prepared the same way each time, and filing this one ahead of the date.

## payment_received
actions: look_at_data, double_down, log_now
summary: Record it, look at where this client came from, and do more of that.

Record it. Then look at how this client was won and how long they have stayed. Recommend putting effort into the source that produces clients like this one.

## task_done
actions: ship_now, look_at_data, share_with_team
summary: Get it in front of people now, and watch what they do with it.

Acknowledge it. Recommend putting it in front of clients or users at once rather than polishing further, and choosing what to watch to know whether it worked. Tell the team it shipped.

## missing_info
actions: look_at_data, ask_one_question
summary: Look for the answer in the records first; ask only what the data cannot tell.

Check what the records already show. Ask the one remaining question, plainly.

## quick_pick
actions: run_test, offer_options, decide_now
summary: If it can be tried small, try it; otherwise pick the option that is easiest to change later.

Where two options can be tested on a small scale, recommend that and name the measure. Otherwise lead with the option that keeps the most room to change course, and list the others.

## discount_request
actions: ask_customer, look_at_data, offer_terms, run_test
summary: Find out what is behind it, look at what this client is worth over time, and trade price only for commitment.

Ask what is driving the request. Look in the records at how long this client has stayed and how much they bring. For a client worth keeping, recommend offering better terms in exchange for a longer commitment rather than a plain cut. If price comes up often, recommend testing a different price on new quotes.

## hiring_decision
actions: hire_contract_first, hire_for_taste, decide_now
summary: Keep the team small: start with a trial project, hire only someone you would work for, and decide quickly.

Ask whether the work justifies a permanent seat. Recommend starting with a short paid project. Hire only if this is someone the founder would be glad to work for. Once that is clear, decide fast; good people do not wait.

## deal_won
actions: look_at_data, double_down, ask_referral, invoice_now
summary: Work out how this deal was won, do more of exactly that, and ask them who else should hear from you.

Acknowledge it. Look at where this deal came from and how long it took. Recommend repeating that route, and asking the client for an introduction while the goodwill is fresh. Offer to raise the invoice.

## milestone
actions: share_with_team, look_at_data, raise_the_bar
summary: Tell the team, look at what drove it, and set the next number.

Share it with the team first. Then look at what produced it, and set the next target as a number with a date.

## record_high
actions: look_at_data, double_down, share_with_team
summary: Find out what caused it, put more behind that, and check that it lasts.

State the record from the records. Look at what was different this time: a client, a channel, a kind of work. Recommend putting more behind it, and watching the following period to see whether it holds.

## deal_lost
actions: look_at_data, ask_customer, run_test
summary: Check whether it is one loss or a pattern, ask what decided it, and test a change on the next quotes.

Acknowledge it. Look across the recent deals that were lost for what they share: a stage, a price point, a kind of client. Recommend asking this client what decided it, then trying one change on the next few quotes and measuring whether more of them close.
