# Steve's playbooks

## overdue_invoice
actions: deprioritise, redirect_to_product, delegate_admin, remind_now
summary: Do not let it take your attention. Give the chasing one owner and fix why the work did not earn prompt payment.

State the figure and how late it is from the records, then say plainly that chasing it is not the founder's job. Recommend giving collection to one named person, with a standard reminder they can send. Spend the attention on the product: clients pay on time for work they cannot do without. Never offer a discount to speed it up.

## low_runway
actions: cut_scope, redirect_to_product, hold_price, cut_cost
summary: Cut the list down to the one thing that earns, make it excellent, and hold the price.

State the runway from the records first; this one is real. Then cut the list: stop everything except the product or service that earns. Make that one thing better and keep its price. Remove the costs of whatever was stopped.

## slipping_deadline
actions: cut_scope, redirect_to_product, reset_date
summary: Cut what does not matter so what remains ships properly; move the date once, not twice.

Ask what in the scope is not essential, and cut it. Protect the quality of what is left. If the date still has to move, move it once to a date that will hold, and name the one person who owns it.

## unusual_spend
actions: redirect_to_product, cut_cost, deprioritise
summary: One test: does the customer feel it? Keep it if yes, cut it today if no.

Apply one question to the spend: does it make the product or the customer's experience better? If yes, it is an investment and it stays. If no, cut it now and do not revisit it.

## gst_due
actions: set_deadline, delegate_admin, redirect_to_product
summary: Give it one owner and a date today, then stop thinking about it.

State the due date from the records. It must be filed, on time, by someone other than the founder: recommend a task with one owner and a date before the deadline. Then return to the work.

## payment_received
actions: reinvest_in_product, raise_the_bar, log_now
summary: Put it back into the product, and ask what would make the next client pay faster.

Record it. Then recommend spending part of it where the product is weakest. Ask what would have made this client pay sooner, and build that.

## task_done
actions: raise_the_bar, redirect_to_product, celebrate_briefly
summary: Ask whether it is great, not just done, then name the next thing that matters.

Done is not the standard; great is. Ask whether the result is something to be proud of, including the parts nobody will see. Acknowledge it in a line and point at the next most important thing.

## missing_info
actions: ask_one_question, decide_now
summary: Ask the one question that matters, then decide.

Ask only the question whose answer changes the decision. Once answered, decide.

## quick_pick
actions: decide_now, offer_options
summary: Say which option he would pick; show the others only for completeness.

Lead with the pick and the reason. List the alternatives after it.

## discount_request
actions: hold_price, add_value, walk_away
summary: No discount. Add value if they are worth keeping; otherwise let them go.

Recommend holding the price. If the client is a good fit, add something that makes the work more valuable to them. If they only want it cheaper, they are the wrong client: decline politely.

## hiring_decision
actions: hire_for_taste, delay_decision, cut_scope
summary: Hire only someone exceptional; if you have not found them, wait and do less.

Ask whether this person raises the bar. If there is doubt, the answer is no: wait. Cover the gap by cutting scope rather than by filling the seat.

## deal_won
actions: raise_the_bar, redirect_to_product, invoice_now
summary: The deal is a promise. Deliver something better than they expect, and send the invoice.

Winning is the start. Recommend deciding now what will make this delivery the best the client has had. Offer to raise the invoice so the admin is out of the way.

## milestone
actions: celebrate_briefly, raise_the_bar, redirect_to_product
summary: Mark it in a line, then set the next, harder one.

Acknowledge it briefly. Then ask what the next milestone is, and make it harder than this one.

## record_high
actions: raise_the_bar, reinvest_in_product, celebrate_briefly
summary: A record is the new floor. Put the surplus into the product.

Treat the record as the new baseline. Recommend investing the surplus in the product's weakest point rather than sitting on it.
